# Regulatory Notes

MediChain is designed as a coordination and settlement layer between diagnostic laboratories, licensed suppliers, and logistics partners. It is not a pharmaceutical distributor and does not itself verify, license, manufacture, import, store, or dispense regulated products.

The initial scope is limited to selected temperature-sensitive diagnostic reagents and test kits. Vaccines and general medicines are out of scope. Product and participant eligibility must be confirmed before any pilot activity.

## Responsibility boundaries

- Suppliers remain responsible for applicable product authorisations, NAFDAC and PCN requirements where relevant, lawful sale/distribution, product quality, and required records.
- Laboratories remain responsible for appropriate procurement, receipt, storage, use, and applicable professional and operational obligations.
- Logistics providers remain responsible for their licensing, handling, custody, transport conditions, and supporting documentation.
- The protocol's registry and attestations are workflow aids. A stored licence-document hash or on-chain verifier attestation is not a legal determination and does not replace checking the underlying licence, its scope, and its validity.
- Temperature records and proof-of-condition hashes do not replace required quality systems, validated monitoring, chain-of-custody controls, or legally required records.

On-chain records do not replace NAFDAC/PCN compliance. Legal advice is needed before handling products, onboarding real participants, or conducting a pilot. Obtain qualified Nigerian regulatory, privacy, payments, and commercial advice for the proposed product scope and operating model.

## Data and privacy

The protocol should not collect or store patient data. Personal data should be minimised and kept off-chain; public ledger records are difficult to remove. Before collecting participant or operational data, define purpose, lawful basis, access, retention, security, data-subject handling, and cross-border storage arrangements with qualified counsel. Hashes can still be personal or commercially revealing in context and should not automatically be treated as anonymous.

## Payments and settlement

Use test assets on testnet during development. Before any real-money use, assess applicable Nigerian and cross-border payment, virtual-asset, custody, consumer, tax, and commercial requirements. A smart-contract escrow does not itself establish that the proposed payment flow is permitted or that contractual remedies are adequate.

This document is informational, not legal advice. It does not assert that MediChain or any participant is compliant with any law or regulation.

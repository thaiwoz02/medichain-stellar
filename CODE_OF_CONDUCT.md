# Code of Conduct

MediChain is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code; accept good-faith corrections gracefully.
- Make space for different perspectives and communicate professionally.
- Protect sensitive information and avoid sharing personal, patient, or commercial data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information without permission is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy privately to the project maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove content, limit participation, or take other action appropriate to the circumstances. Reports will be handled with discretion to the extent practical.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
